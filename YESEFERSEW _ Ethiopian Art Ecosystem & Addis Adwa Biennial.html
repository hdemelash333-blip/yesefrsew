Call for Papers

Systemic & Institutional Challenges in the Visual Art Sector

YESEFERSEW Journal invites scholars, practitioners, curators, artists, and cultural policy experts to submit original research articles, critical essays, and case studies examining the structural barriers and institutional dynamics shaping contemporary visual art ecosystems—with special focus on African and diaspora contexts.

Submission Deadline: To be announced
Publication Date: To be announced

Suggested Topics Include:

  • Institutional critique of arts funding models in post-colonial contexts
  • Barriers to market access for artists from the Global South
  • Infrastructure gaps in art education and professional development
  • Digital divides and technological access for artists and institutions
  • Policy frameworks for creative economy development in Ethiopia and East Africa
  • Diaspora networks as institutional bridges and their limitations
  • Museum and gallery representation: canon formation and exclusion
  • Intellectual property rights and artist protections in emerging markets
  • Sustainability models for arts organizations in resource-constrained environments
  • Gender, class, and regional disparities in arts access and recognition
Submit Your Abstract

Submission Guidelines

Abstract: 300-500 words summarizing research question, methodology, and contribution

Full Paper: 5,000-8,000 words (for accepted abstracts)

Languages: English or Amharic (with English abstract)

Peer Review: Double-blind peer review process

Publication Fee: No article processing charges (APC-free)

Early Career Researchers Welcome

Mentorship support available for first-time submitters from Ethiopian institutions.
